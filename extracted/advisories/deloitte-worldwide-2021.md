# Deloitte Global Tax Reset — Transfer Pricing Documentation Summary (2021)

This guide compiles essential country-by-country (CbC) reporting and documentation (including master file and local file where applicable) information for 144 jurisdictions around the world. Reviewed and updated as of 30 September 2021.

## Interpretations

- **Secondary filing** generally refers to a local filing obligation imposed on resident entities in an MNE group when the jurisdiction does not receive the CbC report via automatic exchange from the parent or surrogate reporting entity's jurisdiction. Some countries with secondary filing requirements may provide exceptions for FY2016.
- **Local file** is defined as either an OECD local file (prepared in accordance with Annex II to Chapter V of the revised OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines) or transfer pricing documentation prepared under local regulations. Some countries also have disclosure requirements that do not directly relate to the OECD local file.
- **Substantially complies** means the tax authority will accept a report that meets OECD content guidelines, and it will likely meet penalty protection requirements. Additional information may be required upon a transfer pricing audit. Language requirements are not considered when determining whether an OECD master file and local file can provide local documentation compliance. This definition does not take into consideration rules for specific types of transactions such as cost sharing or financing.
- **Submit** refers to a requirement to submit either all or, in certain countries, only parts of the documentation. **Provide** refers to having to provide the documentation upon request. **Contemporaneous** refers to having to prepare documentation by a certain date (usually by the time of filing annual tax returns). Certain countries have rules whereby documentation needs to be prepared by a tax return due date under one scenario but also needs to be submitted under another (e.g., Korea, Mexico and Uruguay); these are listed as having "Contemporaneous + Submit" requirements.
- **Parent surrogate filing** means voluntary filing for Ultimate Parent Entities resident in their jurisdictions of residence that do not yet require it.
- Some countries provide a monetary threshold for filing or preparation of the master file or local file/documentation. For the purpose of this document, it is assumed any filing or preparation thresholds have been met.
- There may be multiple due dates for certain countries applicable for different parts of the master file or local file/documentation. The earliest due date that could apply is listed.

## United States

### Country-by-Country (CbC) Report

- **Status of rules:** Final
- **First effective year starting:** 1 Jul 2016
- **Notification requirement:** No
- **Report due date:** By tax return due date
- **Secondary filing requirement:** No
- **Parent Surrogate Filing Available:** Yes
- **Penalties:** Yes

### Master File (MF)

- **Status of rules:** None to date

### Local File (LF) / Documentation

- **Status of rules:** Final
- **New rules effective starting:** Existing rules apply

### MF & LF / Documentation

- **Does OECD MF + LF provide local dox compliance?** Substantially complies
- **Filing requirement:** Contemporaneous; Provide for LF
- **Due date:** Prepare: 15th April, typically extended until 15th October
- **Penalties:** Yes
