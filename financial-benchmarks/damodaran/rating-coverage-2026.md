# Interest Coverage → Synthetic S&P Rating

Damodaran's published mapping of EBIT-to-Interest coverage to a synthetic S&P / Moody's rating. Used as a compact rating proxy for the EBIT/Interest row in the Financial Capacity table.

The mapping reflects observed coverage ratios across large non-financial issuers at each rating band. Practitioners cite this as a reference for inferring rating implications from a single coverage metric, recognising that real-world ratings depend on a much broader set of factors.
